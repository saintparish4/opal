# Code of conduct

This covers the Opal repository: its issues, pull requests, and reviews, and anywhere else on GitHub the project is discussed.

## What's expected

- Be respectful. Argue with the idea, not the person.
- Assume the other person means well, and say plainly what you mean.
- Keep it about the work: the bug, the design, the code.
- Take a "no" on a pull request or a proposal as a decision about the change, not about you.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, harassment, and personal attacks.
- Slurs, sexual remarks, and comments that demean someone for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pestering: reopening a settled argument again and again, or pressing someone who has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If you see or receive any of that, email [blueskylabx@gmail.com](mailto:blueskylabx@gmail.com). Say what happened and where; a link helps. Reports are kept private.

If the problem is the maintainer, [report it to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## What happens

Opal has one maintainer, who reads every report and decides what to do: edit or remove a comment, lock a thread, or block someone from the repository. Serious or repeated behavior gets a block without a warning first.
